# 04 · Personas

> **Hypothesis.** Proto-personas built from desk research, to be replaced after interviews.

## Priya, 32, parent (primary)
- Bengaluru, works in HR, one child aged 4. Types in Hinglish, reads English.
- Moment: child has a mosquito bite that is swelling, or a small burn from the kitchen.
- Needs: fast answer, confidence it is safe *for a child*, a clear line for "go to the doctor now".
- Frustration: forwards from the family group that contradict each other.

## Arjun, 24, young adult
- Pune, software engineer, lives with roommates. Mostly English.
- Moment: sore throat, acidity after late dinners, can't sleep, neck stiff from a laptop.
- Needs: quick, practical steps; low tolerance for lectures.

## Kamla ji, 67, grandmother and family advisor
- Lucknow. Hindi-first; prefers voice.
- Moment: wants to check whether the remedy she suggests is right, or a family member asks her.
- Needs: respect for what she knows; plain Hindi; large text.
- Risk: feeling dismissed. GharGyaan must never mock tradition.

## Non-persona (explicitly not served in V1)
Someone with a chronic illness managing medication, pregnancy care, or mental-health crises beyond signposting. These are routed to professionals.
