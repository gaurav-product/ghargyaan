## The gap we are betting on
**Fragmented everyday knowledge + no evidence labelling + little safety context + weak Indian localisation + poor multimodal experience.**

No single alternative combines: respect for tradition, an honest evidence label, deterministic escalation, and Hinglish input.

## Where competitors are stronger
- Health websites: depth and clinical review processes.
- General AI assistants: breadth, fluency, any-topic coverage.
- YouTube: trust through familiar faces and regional languages.

## Implication
GharGyaan should not compete on breadth. It competes on **trustworthy narrowness**: a small set of situations done safely, with visible sources and an evidence ladder.
