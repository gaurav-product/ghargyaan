# 05 · Jobs to be done

1. **When** something minor happens at home, **I want to** know what to do right now, **so I can** handle it calmly without over- or under-reacting.
2. **When** a family elder suggests a remedy, **I want to** check if it is safe without disrespecting them, **so I can** follow tradition where it helps and avoid harm.
3. **When** I'm not sure how serious something is, **I want** a clear signal about when to see a doctor, **so I** don't wait too long or panic unnecessarily.
4. **When** it's my child (or my parent), **I want** advice that fits their age, **so I** don't apply adult advice to a baby.
5. **When** I have a household problem (stain, smell, mould), **I want** a safe method, **so I** don't mix chemicals or ruin fabric.

## Anti-jobs
- "Tell me what disease I have."
- "Give me a medicine and a dose."
- "Tell me I don't need a doctor."
